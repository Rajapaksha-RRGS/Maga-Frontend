Work Hours & Overtime (OT) Calculation Logic1.

General Rule: Lunch Deduction (Applies to all days)Effective Hours = $\text{Total Hours} - \text{Lunch Deduction}$ Lunch deduction rules based on Total Hours:Up to 12 hours: Deduct 1 hour ($12 \rightarrow 1$). Up to 22 hours: Deduct 2 hours ($22 \rightarrow 2$). 2. Normal Days (Weekdays)Day Status (Shift Attendance):Effective Hours $\le$ 6: Counted as Half Day (0.5). Effective Hours > 6: Counted as Full Day (1.0). Overtime (OT) Calculation:If Full Day: Base standard is 8 hours. $\text{OT} = \text{Effective Hours} - 8$ $> 8 \text{ hrs} \rightarrow +\text{OT}$ $< 8 \text{ hrs} \rightarrow -\text{OT}$ (deficit/deduction) If Half Day: Base standard is 4 hours. $\text{OT} = \text{Effective Hours} - 4$ $> 4 \text{ hrs} \rightarrow +\text{OT}$ $< 4 \text{ hrs} \rightarrow -\text{OT}$ (deficit/deduction) 3. SaturdaysDay Status (Shift Attendance):No Half Day option. Any attendance is counted as a Full Day. Overtime (OT) Calculation:Base standard is 5.5 hours. $\text{OT} = \text{Effective Hours} - 5.5$ $> 5.5 \text{ hrs} \rightarrow +\text{OT}$ $< 5.5 \text{ hrs} \rightarrow -\text{OT}$ 4. Sundays / Public Holidays / Poya DaysDay Status (Shift Attendance):No Regular Day (No Full Day / No Half Day counted). Overtime (OT) Calculation:Entire work time counts directly as Overtime without any baseline deduction. $\text{OT} = \text{Effective Hours}$
